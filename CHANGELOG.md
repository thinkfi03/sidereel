# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (preview)

- Fix: logo and Weekly Ledger images on the Marketplace and in the Extensions view.

## 0.1.0 (preview)

- Sidecar window for short-video sites, pinned to the right edge of the screen.
- macOS: native floating window (Apple Silicon and Intel) that stays above full-screen apps.
- Windows: Edge/Chrome app window kept always on top.
- One flick = one video; `↓`/`j`, `↑`/`k` keys.
- Boss key: pause and hide instantly, bring back without stealing focus.
- Status bar tally of time watched and lines written.
- The Weekly Ledger: a shareable 2400×1350 card with a star-chart of the week.
